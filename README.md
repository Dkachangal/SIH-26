# SIH-26
Marginalised artisans, and craft persons suffer sever revenue draughts in many months.
This can be solved by bringing them to online market place. But having a huge technological gap, like lac of good photography skills, language barriers etc causes drifts them apart. 

# The solution is Kalaa Setu.
This is not just an app that links businesses/consumers and artisans, rather a fully designed and thought end to end system made by giving special focus to the literacy level, and other barriers the marginalised artisans face. 

It has many features like
1) AI-Image enhancer that keeps the product image intact, while enhancing background and colors.
2) AI-Description writer. The artisan can speak up about the product in local language, that would be converted to well structured statements in widely used languages like english.
3) Multi language support. Currently the app supports languages like Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Gujrati, English. More languages are being added constantly.

The artisan is just expected to click and load image (roughly clicked would work well), speak about product in their own language, and enter price they want, AI would suggest them price too.
only these 3 things, and click on upload button. 
That's it.

Automatically the image processing, text processing, catalogue, description writing would happen. 

# The Business View
When Buyers (consumers/businesses) would login, they would see a full marketplace, with category wise division of all the products.
1) They can add to card, chat / contact the artisan or directly place orders.
2) A nominal platform fee would be deducted from the buyer per product, for smooth running of the app (this would ensure long term support and self running capability).
3) # CLUSTERING - large businesses have large needs, single artisan cannot fulfill. Clusters of products having similar quality, design, location would be created automatically using the clustering logic.
   Businesses can order from a cluster, that would automatically place order to multiple artisans on it's own. This would make it easier for businesses to come and purchase products, as order fulfilment would be more promising.

KAALA SETU IS AN INITIATIVE :-
1) To bridge the gap between existing/emerging technology, and culture.
2) To bring forward the talent of the Indian.
3) To help Indians purchase original products of local artisans in any corner of the country.
