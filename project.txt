KALAA SETU
AI-Driven Market Linkage and Smart Cataloging Platform for Marginalized Artisans

Problem Statement:
Marginalized artisans, craftsmen and weavers often face seasonal income gaps and
difficulty accessing wider markets. Low digital literacy, language barriers,
limited technical skills and lack of professional product photography and
cataloging make it difficult for them to participate effectively in digital
commerce.

Kalaa Setu aims to provide a simple AI-powered digital platform that converts
basic artisan inputs such as product photographs, native-language voice
descriptions and expected prices into market-ready product catalogues.

--------------------------------------------------
1. PROJECT OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------

Kalaa Setu is an AI-driven digital commerce and market-linkage platform designed
for marginalized artisans and micro-entrepreneurs.

The platform helps artisans create professional digital product catalogues with
minimal technical knowledge and provides access to both B2C and B2B markets.

The system combines:

- AI-powered image enhancement
- Multilingual NLP-based catalog generation
- AI-based product categorization
- Dynamic ML-based pricing assistance
- B2C and B2B marketplace
- B2B artisan clustering
- Buyer-artisan communication
- Offline-first functionality
- Digital product and inventory management


--------------------------------------------------
2. PROPOSED SOLUTION

--------------------------------------------------
3. AI FEATURES
--------------------------------------------------

3.1 AI IMAGE ENHANCEMENT

Artisans can upload basic or low-quality product photographs.

The AI image-processing component improves the presentation of the product
images so that products can be displayed more professionally in the digital
catalogue.

The objective is to reduce the dependency on professional photography skills.


3.2 MULTILINGUAL NLP CATALOGUER

Artisans can provide product descriptions through voice input in their
native language.

The NLP pipeline processes the description and helps generate structured,
market-ready product information.

The system can assist with:

- Product title
- Product description
- Product attributes
- Language translation
- Search-friendly catalogue information


3.3 AI PRODUCT CATEGORIZATION

The system automatically categorizes products based on available product
information.

This helps buyers discover products more easily and improves organization
within the marketplace.


3.4 DYNAMIC ML PRICING ASSISTANT

Kalaa Setu provides a pricing-assistance mechanism for artisans.

The pricing system can consider factors such as:

- Raw material cost
- Labour hours
- Regional wage information
- Product category
- Material
- Product complexity
- Market information

A base price can be calculated from production-related factors, followed by
a market-based adjustment to generate a suggested selling price.

The pricing assistant is intended to help artisans avoid arbitrary pricing
and make more informed pricing decisions.


--------------------------------------------------
4. DIGITAL MARKETPLACE
--------------------------------------------------

Kalaa Setu provides an online gateway where artisan products can be listed
and discovered by buyers.

The platform supports:

- B2C marketplace
- B2B marketplace
- Product catalogues
- Product search and categorization
- Product information
- Artisan storefronts
- Buyer-artisan communication


--------------------------------------------------
5. B2B ARTISAN CLUSTERING
--------------------------------------------------

Individual artisans may not always be able to fulfill large B2B orders.

Kalaa Setu therefore supports clustering of artisans.

Artisans producing similar or complementary products can be grouped into
clusters so that collective supply can be provided to B2B buyers.

This can help with:

- Large orders
- Collective production
- Increased supply capacity
- Wider market participation
- Better access to institutional buyers


--------------------------------------------------
6. ARTISAN-BUYER COMMUNICATION
--------------------------------------------------

The platform provides communication between artisans and buyers.

Socket-based real-time communication can be used for buyer-artisan chat.

This allows buyers to communicate with artisans regarding:

- Product details
- Customization
- Orders
- Availability
- Other product-related requirements


--------------------------------------------------
7. OFFLINE-FIRST FUNCTIONALITY
--------------------------------------------------

Many artisans may have unreliable internet connectivity.

Kalaa Setu therefore follows an offline-first approach.

Important data can be temporarily stored locally using SQLite cache and
synchronized with the backend when connectivity becomes available.

This allows artisans to continue important catalogue and product-related
activities even with intermittent connectivity.


--------------------------------------------------
8. PRODUCT AND CATALOGUE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------

The platform provides digital product management functionality.

Product information can include:

- Product name
- Category
- Craft type
- Description
- Multilingual description
- Material
- Images
- Price
- Stock
- Artisan information

AI-enhanced product information can be stored along with the product
catalogue.


--------------------------------------------------
9. INVENTORY MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------

The system provides inventory management for artisan products.

Inventory functionality includes:

- Stock tracking
- Stock increase
- Stock decrease
- Low-stock detection
- Raw-material information
- Inventory-related notifications

This helps artisans keep track of product availability and avoid problems
caused by inaccurate stock information.


--------------------------------------------------
10. ORDER MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------

The platform supports the digital order lifecycle.

Orders can move through different states such as:

- Pending
- Completed
- Cancelled

The system can track product quantities, stock reservations and order
information.

This allows marketplace transactions to be managed systematically.


--------------------------------------------------
11. PAYMENT AND MARKET LINKAGE
--------------------------------------------------

The platform is designed to support digital payments and online commerce.

UPI/payment gateway integration can be used to facilitate transactions.

Kalaa Setu aims to connect artisans with both individual consumers and
business/institutional buyers.

The system can also provide integration possibilities with external
marketplaces and government-oriented platforms.


--------------------------------------------------

- Users

- Products
- Categories
- Inventory
- Orders
- Marketplace listings
- Clusters
- Buyer-artisan interactions

The database structure is designed to support scalable product and marketplace
operations.


--------------------------------------------------
13. SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------

The backend uses API-based security mechanisms.

JWT (JSON Web Token) authentication can be used for secure user authentication
and authorization.

Role-based access can distinguish between different types of users such as:

- Artisan
- Buyer
- Admin

API security and controlled access are used to protect user and marketplace
data.
